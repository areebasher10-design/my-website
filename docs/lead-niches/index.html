Lead Niches for Focused Outreach

A niche is a way to organize research and outreach around a shared property or ownership characteristic. Choose the niches that fit your business, and your VA works the campaign using the criteria you agree with us.

Property records and ownership indicators help guide research. They do not establish an owner's circumstances or willingness to sell. Not every niche is available in every market, and records can be incomplete, delayed, or inaccurate.

  • Ownership patterns
  • Property condition
  • Listing status
  • Financial & life-event records
  • Property types

Category 1

Ownership Patterns

Campaigns built around how long, how, and where an owner holds a property.

Absentee owners

What is researched
Properties where the owner's mailing address differs from the property address.
Campaign fit
Useful for reaching out-of-area owners who may be managing a property remotely.

High-equity properties

What is researched
Estimated equity based on available sale history and recorded loan information. Estimates are indicators, not appraisals.
Campaign fit
Helps investors focus on owners who may have more flexibility in how they sell.

Long-term owners

What is researched
Length of ownership based on the most recent recorded transfer.
Campaign fit
Suits agents and investors reaching owners who may be considering a move after many years.

Rental owners and tired-landlord campaigns

What is researched
Indicators that a property may be non-owner-occupied, such as absentee ownership or ownership of multiple properties.
Campaign fit
Lets you ask landlords whether they have any interest in selling a rental—without assuming their experience.

Category 2

Property Condition

Campaigns focused on properties that may need attention or are not currently occupied.

Vacant properties

What is researched
Available vacancy indicators, such as data flags or client-supplied drive-by lists.
Campaign fit
Helps reach owners of properties that appear unoccupied. Occupancy is confirmed only through conversation or inspection.

Code violations

What is researched
Code enforcement records where a jurisdiction makes them available.
Campaign fit
Can identify owners who may be weighing repair costs against other options. Records may be outdated or already resolved.

Distressed-condition properties

What is researched
Condition indicators from available data, client notes, or driving-for-dollars lists.
Campaign fit
Suits investors whose model includes renovation. Condition is described by the owner and verified by your team.

Category 3

Listing Status

Campaigns reaching owners who have already shown some interest in selling.

Expired and withdrawn listings

What is researched
Listings that expired or were withdrawn, using sources available to the client.
Campaign fit
Common for agents offering a fresh approach. Your team is responsible for following applicable listing and solicitation rules.

For-sale-by-owner properties

What is researched
Publicly advertised FSBO properties and the contact method the owner chose to publish.
Campaign fit
Lets agents and investors introduce themselves to owners already selling on their own, respecting any stated contact preferences.

Category 4

Financial & Life-Event Records

These campaigns may reach people during difficult times. Outreach is calm, respectful, and never pressuring—and a request to stop contact is recorded and honored.

Pre-foreclosure

What is researched
Recorded notices, such as notices of default or lis pendens, where available.
Campaign fit
Lets owners know that options may exist. A filing does not mean a foreclosure will occur or that the owner wants to sell.

Callers avoid assumptions about an owner's finances and do not offer legal or financial advice.

Probate and inherited properties

What is researched
Probate filings and inheritance-related records where publicly available.
Campaign fit
Supports gentle outreach to personal representatives or heirs who may need to decide what to do with a property.

Conversations begin with respect for the family's loss. Timing and follow-up follow the person's wishes.

Tax-delinquent properties

What is researched
Property tax records where a jurisdiction publishes delinquency information.
Campaign fit
Can surface owners who may be reconsidering a property. Balances may already be paid or under arrangement.

Callers don't reference an owner's tax situation unless your approved script calls for it.

Category 5

Property Types

Campaigns defined by the kind of property rather than the owner's situation.

Vacant land

What is researched
Parcel size, zoning or land-use codes, and ownership where records provide them.
Campaign fit
Suits land investors and builders reaching owners of lots and acreage.

Small multifamily properties

What is researched
Unit counts, property use codes, and ownership details for 2–4 unit and small apartment properties.
Campaign fit
Helps investors building rental portfolios reach small multifamily owners.

Commercial properties

What is researched
Property use, entity ownership, and available contact details for commercial parcels.
Campaign fit
Supports brokers and acquisition teams reaching owners of commercial property, often through registered entities.
