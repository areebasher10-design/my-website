Campaign workflow

How a Campaign Flows

Every campaign moves through the same five stages. Scroll to follow each one.

  1. Target Market
  2. Property Data
  3. VA Outreach
  4. Qualification
  5. Follow-Up

  1. 01 Target Market

    We start with where you work and who you want to reach: locations, property types, and the owner situations that fit your business model.

  2. 02 Property Data

    Your VA works from skip-traced data you provide, or from research support we agree with you. Records guide outreach; contact details are subject to verification.

  3. 03 VA Outreach

    Your dedicated VA calls on the agreed schedule using your approved scripts and handling instructions, and records each outcome and contact preference.

  4. 04 Qualification

    When a prospect is willing to talk, the VA notes what they choose to share—property details, condition, timeline, and the next step they'd like.

  5. 05 Follow-Up

    Requested callbacks, appointments, and handoffs are organized in your CRM so your team knows exactly what to do next.

Dedicated Real Estate Virtual Assistants

A dedicated VA supports your agreed campaign and workflow. Clients can discuss five-hour or eight-hour daily calling schedules, with flexibility based on their requirements.

What it is

An assigned virtual assistant who works on your outreach campaign on an agreed schedule, following your instructions.

What the VA does

  • Works your campaign on the agreed days and hours
  • Follows your scripts, criteria, and handling rules
  • Documents activity so you can review progress

Who it helps

Investors, agents, wholesalers, brokers, and acquisition teams who need consistent daily prospecting.

Outbound Calling

Real estate cold calling support using agreed target criteria, scripts, and handling instructions. Each call outcome—no answer, wrong number, not interested, callback requested, conversation held—is documented, along with any contact preferences the person expresses.

Contact preferences, including requests not to be called again, are recorded so your team can honor them. Clients remain responsible for their campaign's compliance with applicable calling and marketing rules.

What it is

Consistent outbound dialing to the property owners in your campaign list.

What the VA does

  • Dials your agreed list during scheduled hours
  • Uses your approved scripts and instructions
  • Logs outcomes and contact preferences

Who it helps

Teams that want outreach to happen every day without pulling agents or acquisition managers off their own work.

Seller Lead Qualification

Your VA gathers the information a prospect is willing to share, such as:

  • Property type
  • Condition and occupancy
  • Reason for considering a sale
  • Preferred timeline
  • Price expectations, when appropriate
  • Decision-makers
  • Requested next step

Self-reported vs. verified. Notes record what the prospect said. They are kept separate from verified property facts, such as public-record details, so your team knows what still needs confirming.

What it is

A structured conversation that captures the information your team needs to decide on a next step.

What the VA does

  • Asks your agreed qualification questions
  • Records answers clearly as self-reported
  • Flags prospects that meet your criteria

Who it helps

Investors and acquisition teams who want to spend their time on conversations that fit their criteria.

Appointment Setting

When a prospect agrees, your VA arranges a conversation with your team—by phone, video, or in person, as you prefer—and records the time, contact details, and context for the handoff.

We arrange appointments only with a prospect's agreement. We can't guarantee attendance or that a conversation leads to a completed transaction.

What it is

Real estate appointment setting that places agreed conversations on your team's calendar.

What the VA does

  • Offers times from your availability
  • Confirms details with the prospect
  • Adds notes so your team is prepared

Who it helps

Agents, brokers, and investors who want their time spent in scheduled conversations rather than dialing.

Lead Follow-Up

Many owners aren't ready on the first call. Your VA organizes requested callbacks and follow-up tasks for prospects who are open to continued contact, so no agreed follow-up is forgotten.

What it is

Organized, permission-based follow-up and nurturing.

What the VA does

  • Schedules callbacks at the time requested
  • Tracks follow-up tasks in your system
  • Updates status after each contact

Who it helps

Any team with a pipeline of "not yet" prospects who asked to hear back later.

Skip Tracing and Property Research

You may provide your own skip-traced data, or discuss skip tracing and property-owner research support with Property Link Partners. Research can help identify property details and possible owner contact information for your campaign.

Contact data is subject to verification, and records can be incomplete or out of date. Skip tracing is not assumed to be included in hourly or monthly pricing; any data arrangement is confirmed during consultation.

What it is

Property owner research and contact lookup to prepare records for outreach.

What the VA does

  • Reviews property and ownership records
  • Prepares records in your agreed format
  • Notes data that needs verification

Who it helps

Clients who don't yet have a skip-traced list, or who want help preparing one.

CRM Support

Real estate CRM support that keeps your records usable: notes, statuses, tasks, and handoffs organized within the CRM workflow you agree with us.

What it is

Ongoing data entry and organization in the CRM your team already uses.

What the VA does

  • Logs call notes and outcomes
  • Updates lead statuses and stages
  • Creates follow-up tasks and handoffs

Who it helps

Teams whose pipeline depends on accurate, current CRM records.

Buyer-List and Disposition Support

Organization of your buyer relationships and support for disposition outreach.

  • Supports outreach on opportunities you're authorized to share

Wholesalers and investors who need an organized disposition process.
