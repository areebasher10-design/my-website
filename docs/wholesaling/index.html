Property Opportunities Through Real Estate Wholesaling

How wholesaling differs from our VA services

VA services

Supporting a client

When our VA calls on behalf of a client's campaign, we are providing a service to that client. The client's business is the one pursuing the property.

Wholesaling

Acting in our own transaction

On this page, Property Link Partners is the party pursuing the property. We identify ourselves and explain our role when we discuss one of our own opportunities.

How a transaction can work

From Opportunity to Investor Buyer

Every opportunity is different. This is a general outline of the steps, and of the two ways a transaction may be structured.

  1. Property Opportunity

    We research a property and speak with an owner who is open to discussing a sale.

  2. Contract Review

    If terms are agreed, a written purchase contract sets out each party's rights and obligations.

  3. Transaction Structure

    Depending on the agreement and applicable requirements, the transaction may take one of two paths:

    • Assignment of contractual interest. We transfer our rights under the contract. We do not hold title, and the buyer closes with the owner.
    • Purchase and subsequent sale. We close and take title, then may later sell the property in a separate transaction.

  4. Investor Buyer

    A buyer whose criteria fit the opportunity reviews it and decides independently whether to proceed.

Illustrative only. Our role — contract holder or owner — is disclosed for each opportunity.

For property owners

Considering Selling a Property?

You're welcome to contact us to discuss a potential sale. There is no obligation to accept an offer or to continue the conversation.

  • Tell us about the property and your situation, only as much as you're comfortable sharing.
  • If the property fits our criteria, we may discuss terms. If it doesn't, we'll say so.
  • Before you sign anything, we explain our role and how the transaction may be structured.
  • You're encouraged to review any agreement with an attorney or other advisor of your choice.

We don't buy every property, and we don't promise a particular price, closing date, or type of payment. Any terms are set out in a written agreement.

Our role

Clear About What We Hold

For each opportunity, we explain our role accurately so owners and buyers know who they're dealing with.

When we hold a contractual interest

If we are under contract to buy a property, we hold rights under that contract—not ownership. When we market such an opportunity, we describe it as our contractual interest, and any assignment transfers those contract rights. The buyer then closes with the owner.

When we own the property

If we complete a purchase and take title, we own the property and may later offer it for sale in a separate transaction as the owner.

Transaction-specific disclosures are provided where applicable, including our role in the transaction and any requirements that apply in the property's state. Nothing on this page is legal, tax, or financial advice.
