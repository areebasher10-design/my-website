How it works

From First Call to Documented Follow-Up

  1. Share your market and goals

    Tell us who you want to reach, where you work, and what a useful conversation looks like.

  2. Agree on criteria and data

    Confirm target criteria, scripts, handling instructions, and whether you'll supply data or discuss research support.

  3. Select your VA schedule

    Discuss a five-hour or eight-hour daily schedule, or the arrangement that fits your campaign.

  4. Begin outreach and qualification

    Your VA starts calling, notes what prospects share, and respects contact preferences.

  5. Receive documented activity

    Conversations, callbacks, and next steps are recorded so your team can act.
