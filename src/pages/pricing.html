Pricing Questions

Is skip tracing included?

Skip tracing isn't assumed to be included in either price. You can supply your own data or discuss skip tracing and research support—any data arrangement is confirmed during your consultation.

How many hours does the monthly package include?

The monthly package doesn't come with a preset number of hours. Its schedule and scope are discussed and agreed with you.

Do you guarantee leads or appointments?

No. You pay for dedicated VA support, not a lead quota. Results depend on your market, data, criteria, and campaign—outcomes vary.

How is billing handled?

There's no upfront fee. Billing details are confirmed with you during consultation, before work begins.
