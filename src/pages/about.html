About us

Organized Outreach, Clearly Explained

Property Link Partners connects real estate professionals with dedicated virtual assistant support—and, separately, pursues its own real estate opportunities. We keep those two activities distinct, and we're clear about which one we're doing.

What We Do

{{icon:headset}}

Virtual assistant & lead-generation support

We provide dedicated virtual assistants for real estate investors, realtors and agents, wholesalers, brokers, and property acquisition teams. Our VAs support outbound calling, seller lead qualification, appointment setting, follow-up, property research, and CRM work on each client's agreed campaign.

{{icon:home}}

Independent real estate wholesaling

We also research and pursue select U.S. real estate opportunities for our own account. In those transactions we act for ourselves, and we disclose whether we hold a contractual interest or own the property.
