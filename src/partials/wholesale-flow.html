1. Property Opportunity

    We research a property and speak with an owner who is open to discussing a sale.

  2. Contract Review

    If terms are agreed, a written purchase contract sets out each party's rights and obligations.

  3. Transaction Structure

    Depending on the agreement and applicable requirements, the transaction may take one of two paths:

    • Assignment of contractual interest. We transfer our rights under the contract. We do not hold title, and the buyer closes with the owner.
    • Purchase and subsequent sale. We close and take title, then may later sell the property in a separate transaction.

  4. Investor Buyer

    A buyer whose criteria fit the opportunity reviews it and decides independently whether to proceed.

Illustrative only. Our role — contract holder or owner — is disclosed for each opportunity.
